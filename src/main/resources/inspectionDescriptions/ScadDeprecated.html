Reports modules and argument names from old OpenSCAD versions. Depending on the installed version OpenSCAD reports them as DEPRECATED when rendering or no longer knows them at all: assign(), child(), import_stl(), import_off(), import_dxf(), dxf_linear_extrude(), dxf_rotate_extrude(), import(filename = …), polyhedron(triangles = …) and file/layer of linear_extrude() and rotate_extrude().

The quick fix replaces the name where there is a direct replacement. A module of your own with one of these names is not reported.
